# First Maziss voice test

Your own performances can supply Maziss and, later, other characters. Start with this
short test before recording a whole script: dialogue and delivery will change during
playtesting. These are proposed test lines, not a request for a generic model-training
passage. Keep your natural English accent; we are looking for character, not imitation
of a particular actor.

## File format

- Preferred master: **WAV, PCM, mono, 48 kHz, 24-bit**.
- **16-bit WAV** is completely suitable too; 44.1 kHz recordings are also usable.
- A phone's **M4A** is acceptable for the initial test if that is what you already have.
  Send the original recording rather than repeatedly converting it to MP3.
- One file called `maziss_voice_test.wav` (or `.m4a`) is easiest. Leave about two seconds
  between lines and a few seconds of room silence at the beginning and end.
- Leave the master unprocessed: no music, reverb, pitch effect or heavy noise removal.
  Keep it even if you make an edited version. We can trim and create compressed game
  copies later with free tools.

Use the quietest furnished room available. Keep a steady microphone distance, roughly
15–20 cm if practical, slightly off-axis to reduce breath/popping. Listen to a ten-second
test before recording the rest. If your recorder has a level meter, aim for normal peaks
around -12 to -6 dBFS and avoid clipping/red indicators. Existing equipment is enough;
do not buy anything for this test.

## Character and delivery

Morning after a bad night. Tired, hungover, uneasy, but practically resourceful. His
humour is mostly a defence, not a performance for an audience. Say the lines as thoughts
that occur to him while working. Try two takes: first natural and restrained, then a
little more irritated. You do not need to sustain an exaggerated gravelly voice.

Read only the quoted text, not the ID or direction.

| ID | Direction | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TEST_01` | Quiet realisation; a pause after the first sentence | “They saw me. All three of them.” |
| `TEST_02` | Wincing, distracted by the cushion | “Something under the cushion. That explains my back.” |
| `TEST_03` | Mild recognition while picking up scrap | “There you are. I wondered where I'd put that.” |
| `TEST_04` | Testing a stubborn catch; then a small release | “Come on… there.” |
| `TEST_05` | Reading; then becoming interested | “Paid work outside the city. Transport included.” |
| `TEST_06` | Remembering the restriction | “Of course. I'm not allowed to leave.” |
| `TEST_07` | Practical resolve, with fear underneath | “Right. Find out where they leave from. Worry about the guards after.” |
| `TEST_08` | A short dry observation, not a punchline announcement | “A fine I can pay. Eventually.” |

Optional: a natural sigh, small effort grunt, and short exasperated laugh, separated by
silence. Avoid adding sound effects or other voices behind them.

Upload the original file in chat when ready. We can check noise, clipping, intelligibility
and pacing, then agree Maziss's delivery before recording approved lines in batches.
For other characters, separate files and a brief description of the chosen performance
will help maintain consistency. If another person performs, obtain their permission for
the game's intended editing and distribution before using the recording.

The current prototype remains text-only; no uploaded voice has yet been integrated.
