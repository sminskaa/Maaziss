## Next animation method

- Start with a neutral adult human walk on level ground. Add tiredness/personality only
  after its mechanics are convincing. Preserve Maziss’s first-proposal appearance.
- Develop side-view contact, weight acceptance/down, passing and rise/toe-off poses,
  then the opposite step. Timing comes from normal-speed footage, not an arbitrary
  count of drawn frames or the slow historical GIF.
- Examine heel/toe contact, toe clearance, continuous forward leg swing, weight above
  the support foot, controlled knee flexion, modest hip/chest counter-rotation and
  opposite arm/leg timing. Avoid forcing each joint to follow a sinusoid independently.
- For front/back views, preserve depth: foreshortening and projected leg width change.
  Do not bend a flat 2D knee sideways to simulate motion into or out of the screen.
- Review a simple side-view motion study with frame stepping and the selected reference
  before restoring painted surfaces or implementing more directions. Test moving through
  space, starts and stops after a believable cycle. Numerical checks remain supporting
  evidence; human visual review determines naturalness.
- External tutorials, lab demos and the book are reference material, not bundled game
  assets. The reference-only HTML embeds only the verified public-domain photographic GIF.

## Optional owner contribution

Three short ordinary walking recordings are particularly useful: a side view (both
horizontal directions if possible), toward the camera, and away. Keep the phone still,
landscape, approximately hip height, far enough away to show the whole person and floor
under their shoes throughout. Take 4–6 ordinary steps where space allows; 10–15 seconds
per clip is sufficient. MP4 or MOV, original speed, 1080p at 30fps works; 60fps helps.
Wear ordinary trousers and shoes so knee and foot positions are readable. No exaggerated
limp, drunken acting, special equipment or audio is needed. Send original clips rather
than a slow-motion export. A public video link and relevant timestamps are also useful.
No recording or purchase is required to continue with the verified public references.
