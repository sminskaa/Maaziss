## Actual appearance

The room is readily recognizable. It retains the red worn sofa and blankets on
the left, the mostly straight green door near the centre, the cyan double window
on the right, and the repair desk with its lamp and old device. The noticeboard,
wall basin, hanging clothes, shelf bottles, sill plant and stacked books keep the
inhabitant's practical, untidy identity legible.

Amber lamplight, warm orange floor and plaster, purple shadows, green door and
cool turquoise city view preserve the principal painted colour/light identity.
Dark outlines and textured surfaces remain cartoon-like. Door and window frames
are plausibly constructed, with only modest drawn irregularity.

The output fills a clean rectangular frame. There is no white surround or
pointed cutaway floor tip, and no Maziss or other person is visible. Small white
areas belong to paper and the basin, not a failed background removal. The sofa
and right desk meet the frame edges; some peripheral extremities and shelf
contents are cropped, but their main shapes remain readable. The lower image
contains usable visible floor rather than an isolated floating room wedge.

## Departures and recommendation

This is an image-generation redraw/reframe, not a literal pixel crop. Against
the retained original, the camera/framing and relative furniture sizes differ:
the sofa is more prominent, the room is more openly framed without the original
dark foreground silhouettes, and the floor occupies a larger vertical share.
Individual sofa folds, wall papers, floor seams, books, small props and the
distant city arrangement have changed. The broad designs survive; exact prop
registration and original painted pixels do not.

Recommend showing the compact `visual-preview/maziss-room-16x9.html` as the
requested 16:9 room-reference proposal. The colour and furnishing identity are
close enough for owner review, provided it is clearly described as a reframe
proposal. Keep the approved original unchanged. This image supplies neither
room geometry nor a solved 3D camera, navigation floor, collision boundaries or
independently editable props. No new rendering, modeling or image iteration was
performed during this Art check.
